After saving a replacement key, restart only the Quick Trial process.
Do not stop the advanced app on 7860 or nginx on 7861/8081.
If Quick Trial runs in your foreground terminal, stop it with Ctrl+C there.
Then run:

cd /workspace/Text2TactileGraphics-Code
./launch_quick_trial.sh

The launcher loads and exports the private key without displaying it.
Use the RunPod HTTP connection for port 8081 on this pod (Quick Trial title).
An already-running Quick Trial must be stopped before binding its port again.
