Quick Trial launch (existing Python environment; uv is not required)

cd /workspace/Text2TactileGraphics-Code
./launch_quick_trial.sh

The launcher privately loads /workspace/private/gemini.env, checks the key,
sets single_a100_80gb, and listens on 8080. The current pod's nginx forwards
8081 to 8080. Open the RunPod HTTP connection for 8081: title Quick Trial.
7860/7861 belong to the advanced application; leave that service running.
Keep the terminal running. An occupied port causes a clear startup failure.
See QUICK_TRIAL.md and QUICK_TRIAL_VALIDATION.md for verification details.
